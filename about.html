About The Tasty Recipe Co

About The Tasty Recipe Co

The Tasty Recipe Co is a new food blog for home cooks. We publish recipe roundups — collections of dishes built around one occasion, ingredient or meal — so you can find the right recipe for your table without scrolling through dozens of separate posts. Our goal is simple: to make holiday cooking and everyday meals easier, with ideas worth gathering around.

What We Publish

We publish recipe roundups, seasonal food ideas, meal ideas, desserts, drinks, and cooking inspiration for home cooks. We start with the holiday season: Christmas, then the New Year, followed by evergreen recipes for everyday cooking.

Our first article, a welcome article, was published on November 13, 2026. The site is written in American English for cooks in the United States. Measurements in roundups use US customary units — cups, tablespoons, and Fahrenheit.

Our Editorial Approach

We work on one keyword at a time. Each topic is screened on Pinterest before anything is written, and every article is published one at a time and checked live before the next one begins. We would rather publish slowly and carefully than rush out articles that do not meet our standards.

How We Choose Recipes

We choose recipes for our roundups based on usefulness, quality, and variety — dishes that home cooks will actually want to make. We also consider seasonality and reader interest, so our collections fit the occasion, the time of year, and the meals people are looking for.

Image Policy

We use only properly licensed or generated images, with credits where needed. Where a roundup points to dishes from other food blogs, the article credits its sources. Photos are never presented as our own work when they are not, and we never download and republish competitor photos.

Accuracy & Corrections

We check dates, measurements, and claims in our articles before publishing, but mistakes can still happen. If you spot an error, please let us know through our Contact page or by messaging us on Pinterest. We correct mistakes promptly and note the correction on the article.

Contact

We would love to hear from you — whether you have a question, a recipe request, or a correction to report. The best way to reach us for now is through Pinterest; see our Contact page for details.
