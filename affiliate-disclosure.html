Affiliate Disclosure

✦

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, whether The Tasty Recipe Co uses affiliate links, what affiliate links are, and what would happen if that ever changed. The short answer is in the box below — the rest of the page explains it in full.

In short: The Tasty Recipe Co does not currently use affiliate links and earns no commissions from them. If that ever changes, we will say so clearly here and on the relevant page before any affiliate link appears.

On this page

  • 1. Our current position
  • 2. What affiliate links are
  • 3. If that changes
  • 4. How we would disclose it
  • 5. Editorial independence
  • 6. Questions

1. Our current position

The Tasty Recipe Co does not currently use affiliate links, and we earn no commissions from product or recipe links on this site. Links on our pages are there because they are useful or because they credit a source, not because we are paid for them. The Pinterest links on our pages, for example, are there so you can save a recipe — not because anyone pays us when you click them.

2. What affiliate links are

An affiliate link is a special link to a product or a store. If you click it and buy something, the site that linked to it earns a small commission from the retailer, at no extra cost to you. The price you pay does not go up because of the link — the commission comes out of the retailer's share.

Affiliate links are common on food and lifestyle blogs, because they are one of the ways a free site can pay for itself. Many blogs use them, and there is nothing wrong with that, as long as the reader is told clearly. On this site, there are currently no affiliate links at all, so there is nothing to disclose on any individual page.

3. If that changes

If we ever add affiliate links, we will clearly disclose that here and on the relevant page before any affiliate link appears. That means:

  • This page will be updated to say that we use affiliate links, and to explain which programs we belong to.
  • Any page that contains an affiliate link will say so clearly, near the link itself, so you do not have to hunt for the disclosure.
  • The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change on the day the change takes effect.

Any commission we might earn would come from the retailer, not from you, and it would not increase the price you pay.

4. How we would disclose it

Disclosure only works if a reader can actually see it. If we ever use affiliate links, we will mark them in plain language on the page itself — for example, "This page contains affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you." The disclosure will appear before the first affiliate link on the page, not hidden at the bottom, and it will be written in the same plain language as the rest of the site.

5. Editorial independence

Our recommendations are chosen on usefulness and quality, not on commissions. Whether or not a link ever earns us anything, we only include dishes and sources we believe are worth sharing with home cooks. No retailer, brand, or affiliate program can pay for a place in one of our roundups, for a favorable mention, or for a recipe to be included. If a recommendation ever changes because of a commercial relationship, we will say so on the page.

6. Questions
