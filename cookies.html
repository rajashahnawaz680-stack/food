Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short: This website does not set cookies of its own. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary cookies to keep the site running, and links to other sites may set their own.

1. What Are Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies help sites remember preferences, keep pages secure, and understand how visitors use a site.

2. Cookies We Use

This website does not set any cookies of its own, and we do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may set strictly necessary cookies to deliver pages securely and protect the site from abuse. These cookies cannot be used to identify you personally.

When you follow a link to another website, such as Pinterest, that site may set its own cookies under its own cookie policy.

3. How to Manage Cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you refuse cookies or clear them after a visit. Because this site does not rely on cookies, blocking them should not affect how it works.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we add features that use cookies — for example, analytics or a newsletter — this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date above will change.

5. Contact

Questions about cookies? Reach us through Pinterest at @thetastyrecipeco or see our Contact page.
