Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short: We do not collect personal information directly through this website. There are no accounts, comments, or newsletter sign-ups. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs to run and secure the site.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information on this website. There are no user accounts, comments, newsletter sign-ups, or contact forms at this time.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically collects standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the time of your visit. This information is used to operate, secure, and improve the site, and we do not use it to identify you personally.

If you contact us through Pinterest, anything you share there is governed by Pinterest’s own privacy policy.

2. Cookies

This website does not set cookies of its own. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary cookies to keep the site running securely, and links to other sites may set their own cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

3. Third-Party Links

Our pages link to other websites, including Pinterest. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their privacy policies before sharing personal information with them.

4. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal information directly, there is little for us to share or delete. If you have any question about your privacy on this site, you can reach us through our Pinterest profile — see our Contact page.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we add features that collect information — for example, a newsletter or a contact form — this policy will be updated here, and the “Last updated” date above will change.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through Pinterest at @thetastyrecipeco or see our Contact page.
