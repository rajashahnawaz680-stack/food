Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Welcome to The Tasty Recipe Co. This page explains, in plain language, what information is collected when you visit this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it simple on purpose: this is a small food blog, not a data business.

In short: We do not collect personal information directly through this website. There are no accounts, comments, or newsletter sign-ups. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs to run and secure the site, and you can reach us by email or on Pinterest.

1. Who runs this site

The Tasty Recipe Co is a food blog that publishes recipe roundups for home cooks. We do not publish a postal address. The way to reach us about privacy, or anything else, is by email at rajashahnawaz879@gmail.com or on Pinterest at @thetastyrecipeco.

2. Information we collect

We collect almost nothing about you directly. There is no way to create an account on this site, no comment system, no newsletter to join, and no form that asks for your details. The information connected with your visit is limited to the following:

  • Server and hosting logs. Like almost every website, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records standard technical information when a page is requested — such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages visited, and the date and time. These logs are used to operate the site, keep it secure, and understand which pages are popular.
  • Email you send us. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use these only to reply to you.

We do not collect any of the following, because the site has no feature that asks for them:

  • Names, usernames, or passwords — there are no accounts.
  • Comments or messages posted on the site — there is no comment system.
  • Newsletter details — there is no newsletter.
  • Payment information — there is nothing to buy here.
  • Precise location — we do not track where you are.

3. Cookies

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store. Cookies help a site work properly and remember preferences. This website does not set cookies of its own, and we do not use analytics or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary cookies to deliver pages securely and protect the site from abuse, and sites you visit through our links may set their own cookies under their own policies.

You can delete or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not stop you from reading this site, because the site does not rely on cookies to work.

How to control cookies in your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data — block or clear cookies, for all sites or just this one.
  • Safari: Settings (Preferences) → Privacy — block all cookies, or remove stored data website by website.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data — clear data or manage exceptions per site.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data — block third-party cookies or clear what is stored.

Most browsers also let you browse privately (incognito), so cookies are deleted when you close the window. See our Cookie Policy for the full detail.

4. Analytics

We do not use analytics. This site runs no Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, and no tools that follow you across the web. The only usage information we see is the grouped, technical detail in our hosting logs — for example, which pages were requested and how often — and we do not use it to identify individual visitors. If we ever add an analytics tool, this policy will be updated first, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

5. Advertising

We do not show advertising. There are no ad networks on this site, no sponsored placements, and no advertising cookies. If that ever changes, this policy and our Affiliate Disclosure will say so before any advertising appears.

6. Third-party links and services

Articles on this site link to third-party websites, including Pinterest. When you follow a link and leave The Tasty Recipe Co, the other site's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites, so please check the policy of any external site you open before sharing personal information with it.

The share and save buttons on our pages connect to Pinterest only when you click them. Those services may set their own cookies once you arrive on their platforms, under their own policies.

7. How long information is kept

Hosting logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period for security and operational purposes, and are then deleted or anonymized. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up. Because we collect no other personal information, there is nothing else to retain — and nothing to delete later.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including:

  • EEA / UK (GDPR): the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA / CPRA): the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, and to opt out of its "sale". We do not sell personal information.
  • Everywhere: the right to withdraw cookie consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser.

To exercise any of these rights, email rajashahnawaz879@gmail.com. Because we hold almost nothing about you, most requests can be answered simply: there is nothing to share, correct, or delete. We reply within 30 days.

9. Children's privacy

The Tasty Recipe Co is a general-audience website about food and cooking. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information — for example by email — please contact us at rajashahnawaz879@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site changes — for example, if a new service is added. The "Last updated" date at the top will always show the current version, and significant changes will be reflected on this page before they take effect where the law requires it.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at rajashahnawaz879@gmail.com or message us on Pinterest at @thetastyrecipeco. You can also see our Contact page.
