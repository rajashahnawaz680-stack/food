Editorial Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

In short: We publish original recipe roundups, one article at a time, after screening each topic on Pinterest and checking the finished article live. We do not copy recipes, invent facts, or publish sponsored content or affiliate links without clearly saying so.

1. How We Create Content

We work on one keyword at a time. Each topic is screened on Pinterest before an article is written, and articles are published one at a time — each one is checked live before the next one begins. This keeps our publishing slow, deliberate, and focused on quality.

2. Editorial Standards

Our descriptions are written in our own words. We do not copy recipes or article text from other sites, and we do not fabricate cooking times, temperatures, or nutrition figures. If we do not know something, we do not present it as fact.

3. Sourcing

Our roundups point to real dishes from real sources, and those sources are credited in the article. We never imply that we created a recipe that belongs to someone else.

4. Recipe Selection

We choose recipes based on usefulness, quality, and variety, as well as seasonality and reader interest. Our goal is a collection that helps a home cook find the right dish for the occasion.

5. Fact-Checking

Before an article is published, we check dates, measurements, and claims in it. We do not invent testing, credentials, or reviews, and we do not claim experience we do not have.

6. Corrections

If you spot a mistake, please report it by messaging us on Pinterest or through our Contact page. We correct mistakes promptly and note the correction on the article.

7. Image Rights

We use only properly licensed or generated images, with credits where needed. We never download and republish competitor photos, and we never present an image as our own work when it is not.

8. Sponsored Content

We do not currently publish sponsored content. If that changes, any sponsored article or placement will be clearly labelled as sponsored here and on the relevant page.

9. Affiliate Disclosure

We do not currently use affiliate links. For full details, see our Affiliate Disclosure page.

10. AI Assistance

AI may assist with drafting, organization, and formatting our content. Every article is reviewed and edited by our team before it is published. We do not publish mass-produced spam, and we do not fabricate experiences or quotes.
